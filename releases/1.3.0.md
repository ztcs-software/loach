**What's new**
- **Chat folders.** Drag one chat in the sidebar onto another, give the folder a name, and both are filed together. Drop chats in and out, rename or delete the folder.
- **Colour labels.** Tag any chat Red, Amber, Green, Blue, Purple or Pink from its **Label** menu. The dot follows the chat everywhere it's listed — sidebar, Space view and chat menus.
- **Pinned responses.** Pin any reply from its `…` menu and it lands in a **Pinned** bar under the chat header. Click a chip to jump straight back to that answer. 
- **Share a message.** Copy any message as text or as a rendered chat-bubble image, save the image as a PNG, or open a pre-filled post on Facebook, X, Reddit or LinkedIn. Loach uploads nothing — sharing hands text to your browser or leaves an image on your clipboard.
- **Start Ollama from Loach.** When the daemon isn't answering, the model picker offers a **Start Ollama** button. **Settings → Providers** can also start it automatically at launch — opt-in, and only for an Ollama running on this computer.
- **Auto-check for updates.** Switch it on in **Settings → Updates** and Loach checks once per launch, showing an **Update available** dialog with the new version's notes. Nothing downloads until you choose Update now.

**Fixes**
- **No more duplicate sends.** Hitting send twice in quick succession can no longer dispatch the same message twice, and compaction no longer hangs partway through.
- **Fresher model and Space data.** Switching endpoints or editing a Space no longer leaves stale entries behind, and per-chat parameters merge correctly instead of clobbering your defaults.
- **Steadier upgrades.** Interrupted database migrations no longer leave the app unable to start, and MCP servers stop needlessly reconnecting.
- Assorted stability, security and accessibility hardening throughout the app.
